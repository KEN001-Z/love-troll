About this demo

VYBE is a frontend prototype built with HTML, CSS and JavaScript. It has no backend, so there are no real accounts, matches, messages or notifications.

Demo terms

Everything is for entertainment. Profiles are invented adults, and quiz and compatibility results are jokes, not predictions or psychological assessments.

Demo privacy notice

Anything you type is processed in your browser only. Passwords and emails are never sent, and are not stored in cookies, localStorage or sessionStorage. Refresh the page and your demo profile disappears.
